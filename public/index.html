THE TABLE RULES

A little luck.
A little strategy.

  1. Roll together. The active player rolls two white dice and every unlocked color die.
  2. White dice first. Everyone may cross out their total in one row, or pass. The table waits for everyone.
  3. Then a little color. The active player may add one white die to one colored die and cross that total in that color, or pass.
  4. Always move right. Skipped numbers on the left are gone for good. Tap a number, then confirm to cross it out.
  5. Earn the lock. Cross at least five other numbers in a row before its last number. Crossing the last number also crosses the lock. The lock counts toward scoring, and that color die leaves play.
  6. Every cross counts. A row with n crosses scores n × (n + 1) ÷ 2. Each penalty costs 5. Only the active player takes a penalty if they pass both steps.
  7. Finish the turn. The game ends after the turn that locks a second color or gives the active player a fourth penalty. Highest score wins; equal scores share the win.

A color locked during the white step closes for everyone after all white choices are in. Keep the game open or return on the same browser to reclaim your seat.
